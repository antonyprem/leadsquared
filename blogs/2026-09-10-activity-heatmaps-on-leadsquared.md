1. Feature Overview Activity Heatmaps in Field Force Insights provide a geographic view of where your field teams perform activities over a selected period. By transforming activity data into a color-coded density map, the feature helps managers quickly identify high-activity hotspots, coverage gaps, and under-served areas.
